Roofing in Buffalo, NY

Professional roofing built for Buffalo's tough winters

Trusted Roofing in the Buffalo Area

Buffalo is the heart of Erie County and one of the snowiest cities in the United States. Between heavy lake-effect storms rolling off Lake Erie, ice buildup through the winter months, and strong winds that can batter any roofline, properties here need roofing that can take a beating year after year.

That's exactly what a durable, well-installed roof delivers. Troyer's Contracting serves homeowners and businesses throughout the Buffalo metro area — from the city's historic neighborhoods near Elmwood and Allentown to the growing suburbs in Cheektowaga, Amherst, and Tonawanda. Whether you own an older home that needs a durable upgrade or you're building new, metal roofing is the smartest long-term investment for this climate.

Buffalo's building stock is diverse. You'll find century-old Victorians, mid-century ranches, modern infill construction, and large commercial properties all within a few miles of each other. We've worked on all of them. Our team understands how to handle the unique challenges each building type presents, from complex roof lines on historic homes to flat commercial roofs on warehouse buildings.

Our Services in Buffalo

We offer a complete range of roofing and construction services to residential and commercial properties across the Buffalo area. Every project uses materials and techniques suited for Western New York's demanding climate.

Standing Seam Metal Roofs

The premium option for Buffalo homeowners who want a sleek, modern look with the best weather protection available. Concealed fasteners and interlocking seams create a watertight barrier against lake-effect storms.

Corrugated Metal Roofs

A cost-effective, reliable choice for homes, garages, barns, and agricultural buildings in the greater Buffalo area. Lightweight yet strong enough to handle heavy snow loads.

Roof Repairs

Buffalo winters are hard on roofs. If you've got storm damage, leaks, or panels that need attention, we'll diagnose the problem and make a lasting repair.

Roof Replacement

Ready to replace aging shingles or a worn-out roof? We manage the entire process — tear-off, inspection, and installation of a new metal roofing system.

Commercial Roofing

From downtown Buffalo office buildings to industrial facilities along the waterfront, we deliver commercial metal roofing solutions designed for durability and low maintenance.

Gutters & Trim

With Buffalo's heavy precipitation, proper water management is essential. We fabricate and install custom gutters, downspouts, and trim to protect your foundation and finish your roofline.

Pole Barn Garages

Extra storage and workshop space is always in demand around Buffalo. Our pole barn garages feature durable post-frame construction with metal roofing and siding.

Decks

Buffalo summers are worth enjoying outdoors. We build custom decks in pressure-treated lumber, cedar, and composite materials to extend your living space.
