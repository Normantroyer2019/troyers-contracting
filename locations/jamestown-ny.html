Serving Jamestown and the Chautauqua Lake Region

Jamestown — known as the Pearl City — is one of southern Chautauqua County's most historic communities. Situated at the southern end of Chautauqua Lake, the city features a rich mix of architecture, from Victorian-era homes in the downtown historic district to mid-century neighborhoods and newer residential developments on the outskirts of town.

The climate in Jamestown is distinct from the lakeshore communities further north. While it doesn't get the same lake-effect bands off Lake Erie, the area still sees significant snowfall, bitterly cold winters, and the kind of freeze-thaw cycling that destroys traditional asphalt shingles in a fraction of their rated lifespan. Metal roofing is built to handle these conditions without the constant maintenance and premature replacement that shingle roofs demand.

Troyer's Contracting serves homeowners and businesses throughout the Jamestown area, including properties along Chautauqua Lake, the Falconer area, Lakewood, Celoron, and the surrounding rural communities of southern Chautauqua County. Whether you're updating a century-old home on one of Jamestown's tree-lined streets or building a new garage on a rural lot, we have the experience to deliver a quality result.
