Roofing in Olean, NY

Quality roofing for Olean and the Enchanted Mountains

Serving Olean and Cattaraugus County

Olean is the largest city in Cattaraugus County and serves as the commercial hub for the Enchanted Mountains region of Western New York's Southern Tier. Nestled along the Allegheny River near the Pennsylvania border, the area features a mix of in-town residential neighborhoods, sprawling rural properties, and commercial buildings that support the local economy.

Winters in Olean are no joke. The elevation and inland location mean consistently cold temperatures, heavy snowfall, and ice that can persist for months. Traditional asphalt shingles take a beating under these conditions, often needing replacement well before their rated lifespan. Metal roofing is built differently — it sheds snow under its own weight, resists ice dam formation, and doesn't crack or curl in extreme cold the way shingles do.

Troyer's Contracting proudly serves homeowners and businesses throughout the Olean area. Whether your property is in town near St. Bonaventure University, out toward Allegany State Park, or anywhere in Cattaraugus County, we deliver the same quality craftsmanship and honest service on every project. We also serve nearby communities including Salamanca, Ellicottville, Little Valley, and properties extending into Allegany County.

Our Services in Olean

We provide a full range of roofing and construction services for properties in the Olean area. The mix of residential homes, farms, and commercial buildings throughout Cattaraugus County means we regularly handle a wide variety of project types.

Standing Seam Metal Roofs

The premier choice for Olean homeowners who want long-lasting protection with a clean, modern appearance. Concealed fasteners eliminate exposed screw points that can fail over time.

Corrugated Metal Roofs

A practical, affordable option widely used on homes, barns, and agricultural buildings throughout Cattaraugus County. Durable and available in many colors.

Roof Repairs

Southern Tier winters are hard on any roof. We diagnose and fix leaks, storm damage, and wear so you get a lasting repair — not a temporary patch.

Roof Replacement

If your shingles are failing prematurely in the Olean climate, it may be time to switch to metal. We handle tear-off, deck inspection, and complete installation.

Commercial Roofing

From Olean's downtown businesses along North Union Street to commercial and industrial properties throughout the region, we provide metal roofing built for commercial demands.

Gutters & Trim

Heavy snow and spring melt make proper water management critical in the Olean area. We fabricate and install custom gutters, downspouts, and trim for a complete roofing system.

Pole Barn Garages

The rural properties throughout Cattaraugus County are a natural fit for pole barn garages. We build custom structures for vehicle storage, workshops, and farm equipment.

Decks

The Enchanted Mountains region is beautiful in summer. Extend your outdoor living space with a custom deck built from pressure-treated lumber, cedar, or composite materials.
