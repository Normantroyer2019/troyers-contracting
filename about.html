About Us

Family-owned. Quality-driven. Built on trust.

Our Story

Troyer's Contracting was founded with a simple mission: to provide homeowners and businesses with roofing solutions that are built to last. What started as a small, family-run operation has grown into one of the region's most trusted roofing contractors.

With over 15 years of experience in the roofing industry — from metal and asphalt shingle to repairs and replacements — we've built our reputation on hard work, honest pricing, and exceptional craftsmanship. Every roof we install reflects our commitment to quality, because we know that a roof isn't just a covering, it's the first line of defense for your home or business.

As a family-owned business, we treat every customer like a neighbor. We show up on time, communicate clearly throughout every project, and stand behind our work long after the last panel is in place.
